Floor Age Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

In short: Floor Age does not collect any data. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

Who we are

Floor Age is an iPhone app made by Jeyaraj David, an independent developer ("we"). This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

What we collect

Nothing. We have no server, and the app sends nothing to us or to anyone else. We cannot see your profile, test results, photos, steps, sleep or anything else you do in the app.

What stays on your iPhone

The app saves the following in its own private storage on your device, so it can show your progress:

  • Your profile (such as name, age, gender, height, weight and any limitations you choose), and the same for any family profiles you add
  • Floor Age test results and their history
  • Training sessions you complete, your plan, meals you log, weight entries and sleep entries
  • Your settings, such as reminder time, coach style and voice

This data is included in your normal iPhone backups (iCloud or computer) under your control, like any other app's data. We never receive a copy.

Camera

  • Floor Age check: if you let the camera help score the tests, the app uses Apple's on-device body pose detection to count chair stands, time your balance and estimate your reach. Each camera frame is processed on your iPhone and then discarded. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent.
  • Food photos: when you snap your plate, the photo is checked on your iPhone by Apple's built-in image classifier to suggest foods. The photo is not saved and is never sent anywhere.

You can turn camera access off at any time in iPhone Settings › Floor Age, and enter numbers by hand instead.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Floor Age reads your sleep from Apple Health to show how long you sleep. Access is read-only: the app never writes to or changes your Health data. Health data is used only on your iPhone, is never sent anywhere, and is never used for advertising or shared with anyone.

Motion and steps

Floor Age counts your steps with your iPhone's motion sensors (Motion & Fitness). Step counts are read and shown on your device only.

Notifications

If you turn on the evening reminder, the reminder is scheduled on your iPhone. No notification server is involved.

Purchases

Floor Age Plus is an optional in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple processes the payment under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive your payment details, and the app checks your purchase on the device using Apple's signed receipts. This is the only time the app goes online.

No analytics, ads or tracking

The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or third-party tracking code. It does not track you across other apps or websites. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in iPhone Settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports and usage statistics under Apple's own terms.

Deleting your data

You are in control. To delete everything, open the app and go to Settings › Delete all my data, or simply delete the app from your iPhone. Because we hold no copy, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Children

Floor Age is a general fitness app and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, including children.

Medical note

Floor Age is a fitness estimate, not medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat or predict any disease or condition.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above. If the app ever starts handling data differently, we will say so here before that version is released.
