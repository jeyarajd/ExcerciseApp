Floor Age Plus

How do I restore my Plus purchase?

Plus is a one-time purchase linked to your Apple Account. On a new iPhone, or after reinstalling, open Settings › Restore Purchases in the app (the Plus screen has the same button). Make sure you are signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought it with.

Is Plus a subscription?

No. You pay once and keep it. The Floor Age check, daily coached sessions, steps, calorie log, BMI and all safety guidance are free without Plus.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple, so refunds are too. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple Account.

Family profiles

Can I test my parents or my partner on my iPhone?

Yes, with Plus. Tap the round avatar at the top of the Today tab, add a family member, and switch to them before a test or session. Each person gets their own Floor Age, plan and history. Steps, Apple Health sleep and reminders stay with the iPhone’s owner, because they come from this phone. Everything stays on this iPhone.

Using the camera in the Floor Age check

Tips for accurate camera scoring
  • Place the iPhone about 2–3 metres (7–10 feet) away, propped up at about waist height, so your whole body is in view from head to feet.
  • Chair stand and toe reach: stand or sit side-on to the camera.
  • One-leg balance: face the camera.
  • Use good, even light, and avoid a bright window behind you. Fitted clothes help.
  • Keep other people out of the frame.
  • You can always correct a number by hand. The sit-to-rise test is always scored by you.
Is the camera recording me?

No. Frames are processed on your iPhone by Apple's on-device body pose detection and immediately discarded. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent. Food photos work the same way.

Your data

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Floor Age has no account and no server, and it collects nothing. See the privacy policy.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, go to Settings › Delete all my data. Deleting the app also removes everything. We hold no copy, so there is nothing to delete on our side.

Why aren't my steps showing?

Floor Age needs Motion & Fitness access. Check iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Motion & Fitness and make sure Floor Age is on.

Languages

Which languages does Floor Age speak?

English, Hindi and Spanish, including the spoken coaching. The app follows your iPhone's language. To use a different one just for Floor Age, go to iPhone Settings › Floor Age › Language.

Safety

Floor Age is a fitness estimate, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor before starting a new exercise programme, especially if you have a health condition, and stop if anything hurts. Use a wall or sturdy chair for support where the app suggests it.
