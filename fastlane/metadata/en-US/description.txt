Can you get up off the floor without using your hands?

Floor Age turns that simple move, plus three more quick tests, into one number: the age your body moves like. Then a friendly 3D coach shows you what to work on, about 10 minutes a day, at home.

No account. No ads. No tracking. Everything stays on your iPhone.

FOUR TESTS, ONE NUMBER
• Sit to rise: sit down on the floor and stand back up. It's a well-known fitness test from published research (Araújo et al., 2012).
• One-leg balance: how long you can stand steady on one foot.
• 30-second chair stand: how many times you can stand up from a chair.
• Toe reach: how far you can reach towards your toes.

You get an estimated "equivalent age" for each area and an overall Floor Age, so you know exactly where to start.

YOUR CAMERA KEEPS SCORE
Prop up your iPhone and it counts your chair stands, starts and stops the balance timer, and suggests how far you reached, using Apple's on-device body pose detection. Nothing is recorded or sent: frames are processed on the phone and thrown away. You can always correct a number.

A COACH IN YOUR LIVING ROOM
• A realistic 3D coach (woman or man) demonstrates every move. Turn and zoom to see it from any side, or switch to a friendly cartoon coach.
• Spoken coaching in your iPhone's own voices, even offline and on silent. Your music keeps playing.
• A short daily session built around your weakest areas.
• Moves are skipped or made easier for knee, hip or back problems.
• Missed a day? Your progress never resets.

SHARE THE CHALLENGE
Post a story-sized card of your Floor Age to WhatsApp or Instagram and ask your friends: "Can you get up off the floor without your hands?"

THE BASICS, FREE

• A calorie log with everyday dishes, plus your own foods
• BMI with your healthy weight range and a weight chart
